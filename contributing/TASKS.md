# Scoped contributor tasks for cdid for Stata

Read [Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [contributor skill](../.agents/skills/cdid-contribution/SKILL.md). The maintainer opens issues for specific tasks. Each issue gives the exact execution commit, expected output and checks. Confirm the issue and your assignment before starting.

## Documentation walkthrough

Follow the [installation guide](../docs/installation.md) and [hands-on guide](../docs/hands-on.md). Report broken links, mismatches between commands and printed output, and steps that require clarification. State which steps you ran in Stata and provide the package and Stata versions.

## Missing-outcome terminology

Review [preparing data](../docs/help/prepare-data.md), [investigating missingness](../docs/help/investigate-missingness.md) and [handling missing outcomes](../docs/methods/missing-outcomes.md). Identify ambiguities about the study population, observed pairs or the sample used to fit an observation model. Report the exact passages and suggested wording.

## Missing-panel profiling

Use synthetic data and the workload in the [performance guide](../docs/performance.md). Report complete-call time and the actual sample size, settings, platform and Stata edition. Include refusals and timeouts. Submit measurements before proposing changes to the estimator or implementation.
